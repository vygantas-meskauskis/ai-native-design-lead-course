## Risks I'm watching

| Risk | Signal | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Moving too fast | Team resistance | Listen first; opt-in |
| Seen as "the AI person" | Conversations only about tools | Lead with users and quality |
| Eng sees overstepping | Friction in reviews | Co-own the contract |
| Leadership expects magic | "Can we cut the timeline in half?" | Evidence-based expectations |
| System work stalls | Feature pressure | Tie to pilot and drift data |

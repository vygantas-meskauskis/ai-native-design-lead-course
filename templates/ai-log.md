# AI Log

> Log **every** AI use for the duration of the course (and ideally beyond). Each entry takes about 30 seconds. After 12 weeks you'll have real evidence of where AI helps you, which you can use for your policy, pilot and executive conversations.

## How to use

- One row per AI use.
- **Time saved** = estimated time without AI minus actual time (including prompting and fixing). Negative values are fine and important.
- **Quality** = 1 (unusable) to 5 (better than I'd have done).
- **Verification** = what you checked (none / skim / verify-3 / full).
